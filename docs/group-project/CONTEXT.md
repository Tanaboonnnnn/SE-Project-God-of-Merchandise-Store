# Campus Store — Domain Glossary

This file records agreed domain terminology only; implementation decisions and open requirements belong in `requirements-log.md`.

- **Campus Store:** A fictional university-merchandise ordering business used for the CPE334 project. It is not a claim of an authorized KMUTT store.
- **Product:** A distinct catalog offering or design/type. Sprint 1 has six products: two T-shirt designs, one tote bag, one notebook, one pen and one water bottle. A product can have more than one sellable variant without becoming multiple products.
- **Product variant:** A selectable version of a product. For the two T-shirt products, each size S/M/L/XL is a distinct variant. The other four products have one sellable option each, with no size/color selection.
- **Sellable option:** A distinct purchasable choice for order quantity and stock: a particular T-shirt design-and-size or a standard product without size choices.
- **All-or-nothing order submission:** A customer order request is valid as a whole only when every requested option and quantity can be accepted. Otherwise no Order is created, no items are partially accepted, and no inventory is reserved.
- **Failed order submission:** An attempted order request that does not meet the requirements to create an Order. It creates no persisted Order and is **not** an Operator-Rejected Order, which previously existed as a Pending Order.
- **Order Item (order line):** One recorded line in an Order representing one exact Sellable Option, its combined ordered quantity and its unit-price snapshot. Distinct size variants are distinct Order Items.
- **Duplicate option entry:** An additional submitted line selecting a Sellable Option already listed in the same request; it refers to the same purchasable choice, not a distinct product or size.
- **Normalized Order Item:** The single recorded Order Item that represents all valid submitted entries for one Sellable Option, with their quantities combined. Multiple references to an option do not create multiple persisted lines.
- **Logical order submission:** One customer intention to place one checkout order, regardless of how many times the same submission request reaches the system. It is distinct from a new intentional checkout, even when the selected products and quantities are unchanged.
- **Order submission retry:** A repeat attempt to complete the same logical order submission after a delayed response, timeout or repeated action. It refers to that same intended Order, not a new sale or a new stock reservation.
- **Duplicate whole-order submission:** Multiple received requests representing one logical order submission; unlike duplicate option entries, this repetition is across whole checkout attempts rather than multiple lines within one order.
- **Per-option ordered quantity:** The total whole-unit count requested for the same sellable option in one order, combining repeated references to that option rather than treating duplicated lines as different stock choices. It is checked against the option's Available Stock and the order quantity limit recorded in DEC-023.
- **T-shirt design price:** The unit price associated with one T-shirt design, shared by its S, M, L and XL variants. Two different designs can have different unit prices; choosing a size changes stock selection, not the price within the same design.
- **Unit price:** The price of one sellable unit of a product, before any shipping fee. T-shirt sizes within one design share the design price; other products each have their own unit price.
- **Order-line price snapshot:** The product unit price recorded with a submitted order line, preserving the price at ordering time even if the catalog price later changes; it excludes the separately charged order-level shipping fee.
- **Product subtotal:** The sum of each order line's quantity multiplied by its recorded unit price, before shipping.
- **Order total:** The amount charged for a submitted order, comprising the product subtotal plus its separately calculated shipping fee under the currently confirmed ordering rules.
- **Order status:** The business state of a persisted Order, distinct from any payment information; the currently defined states include Pending, Accepted, Rejected and Cancelled.
- **Simulated payment:** A representation of a payment outcome or state without an actual money transfer. It is distinct from Order acceptance and inventory movements; its future business rules have not yet been defined.
- **Inventory / stock:** The quantities associated with an individual sellable option, distinguished by T-shirt design and size where applicable. Stock for one size cannot satisfy an order for another size. Stock can be available, reserved, or sold.
- **Available stock:** Units of a sellable option that remain available for new customer orders; reserving units reduces this availability.
- **Initial available stock:** The starting sellable quantity assigned to each exact product option when a fresh Campus Store demonstration catalog is seeded, before any units have been reserved or sold; each T-shirt size has its own initial quantity. The quantities themselves are recorded in DEC-022 of the Requirements Decision Log.
- **Reserved stock:** Units allocated to a Pending order awaiting its resolution by Operator acceptance/rejection or cancellation by its Customer. Reserved units cannot be allocated to another order and are not yet sold.
- **Sold stock:** Units committed as sold when an Operator accepts an order, converted from that order's existing reservation.
- **Order acceptance:** The Operator's approval of a pending order, changing its reserved stock to sold stock without reserving additional units.
- **Order rejection:** An Operator's reasoned refusal of a Pending Order before acceptance; the Order becomes Rejected with a recorded explanation, and its previously reserved stock returns to available once. It is distinct from a failed checkout submission or cancellation of an accepted sale.
- **Rejection reason:** The non-blank explanation supplied by an Operator for declining a Pending Order, recorded with that Rejected Order. It can be viewed by the Order's owning Customer and by Operators, but not by other Customers.
- **Reservation release:** The return of an order's previously reserved, unsold units to available stock after Operator rejection or Customer cancellation of a Pending order. Releasing the same reservation more than once does not add further stock.
- **Pending order:** A successfully submitted order whose requested units are reserved and which awaits Operator acceptance/rejection or cancellation by its Customer.
- **Rejected order:** An Order declined by an Operator before acceptance, with a recorded rejection reason visible to the owning Customer and Operators. Its reservation has been released; it cannot subsequently be accepted.
- **Customer cancellation:** An action by a Customer that stops only their own Pending order before Operator acceptance or rejection; it releases the order's reservation. It does not apply to Accepted orders in Sprint 1.
- **Cancelled order:** A previously Pending order stopped by its owning Customer. Its reservation has been released and it cannot subsequently be accepted or rejected.
- **Accepted order:** An order approved by an Operator, whose previously reserved units are now Sold. In Sprint 1 the Customer cannot cancel an Accepted order; any later-sprint post-acceptance cancellation or return policy is not yet defined.
- **Customer:** An authenticated account role that creates and views only its own orders and may cancel its own Pending orders.
- **Operator:** An authenticated staff role that may view all Campus Store orders and accept or reject any Pending order; the role has no individual order assignment or region-specific queue in the agreed Sprint 1 business scope.
- **Store operations scope:** The shared set of orders belonging to the single Campus Store, visible to every Operator regardless of customer, assigned staff member or delivery destination; it does not include orders of any hypothetical other store.
- **Order:** A successfully submitted, persisted customer request containing one or more valid Order Items and delivery details, with server-calculated charges and recorded price-at-order snapshots. Each exact Sellable Option appears at most once among its recorded Order Items. Failed submissions are not Orders. No Order is created with only a subset of the items the Customer requested.
- **Submitted Order contents:** The items and their quantities, unit-price snapshots, recipient/contact details, written delivery address, selected delivery pin and recorded charges fixed when an Order is successfully placed. Subsequent changes to the Order Status do not change these recorded contents.
- **Replacement Order:** A new, independent Order a Customer may place after cancelling their own Pending Order to correct what they intended to buy or where it should be delivered. It is not an edit or continuation of the cancelled Order; it has its own validity, availability, pricing and submission identity.
- **Delivery contact:** The named recipient and telephone number supplied so the order can be delivered and the recipient contacted; the recipient need not be the same person as the authenticated Customer.
- **Structured delivery address:** The written delivery details separated into address detail, subdistrict, district, province and postal code, rather than a single free-text location or a map pin.
- **Delivery-address validity:** Whether the required contact and written-address fields are present and meet the agreed basic format rules. This is distinct from whether the written address is geographically correct or matches the selected destination pin.
- **Delivery destination:** The recipient's structured written address and the destination pin selected on a map; the pin is not automatically proven to match the written address.
- **Selected destination pin:** The delivery location the Customer explicitly chose on the map. Its coordinates may remain selected even when the map can no longer be displayed; selection alone does not verify the written address.
- **Destination reconfirmation:** The Customer's explicit acknowledgement that a previously selected valid pin remains the intended delivery location before checkout continues after a map-loading failure. It is not automatic address validation.
- **Delivery address and pin confirmation:** The Customer's explicit acknowledgement, before each checkout submission, that their displayed written delivery address and selected map pin describe their intended destination. It is a declaration by the Customer, not an automatic determination that the address and coordinates geographically match. Unlike destination reconfirmation, it applies to every checkout, not only when the map fails.
- **Shipping origin:** The simulated Campus Store dispatch point at the approximate center of KMUTT Bangmod campus, with precise coordinates to be verified.
- **Shipping distance:** Straight-line distance from the shipping origin to the customer-selected destination pin, not road-route distance.
- **Shipping fee:** The team's distance-tier charge for an order, calculated server-side from unrounded shipping distance.
